Privacy Policy.

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 26, 2026 DOMAIN: SCRIBE.VASTX.APP
01 / OVERVIEW

Local-First Architecture

Scribe ("we", "our", or "us") operates scribe.vastx.app and its browser-based tools, including Scribe Design Studio and Scribe Convert.

Your designs, media, and documents are processed locally on your device inside your web browser. We do not upload, store, inspect, or sell the files you edit or convert.

02 / DATA WE ACCESS

Google Account Sign-In

When you optionally connect your Google Account via Google OAuth, we only request the minimum permissions needed to authenticate you and support user-initiated features:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your name, email address, and profile avatar to identify your active session in the interface.
  • User-Authorized Actions: Any optional cloud sync or export features you explicitly trigger remain under your direct control.

We never sell your Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third-party data brokers.

03 / STORAGE

Browser Storage & Tokens

Scribe uses your browser's local storage and session memory to keep your workspace preferences, active session state, and local project drafts available on your device.

You can disconnect your Google Account or clear site data in your browser settings at any time to immediately revoke local session tokens.

04 / THIRD PARTIES

External Services

Scribe is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Identity Services for authentication and open-source WebAssembly libraries (such as FFmpeg and PDF.js) loaded via public CDNs to run conversions locally in your browser.

05 / CONTACT

Questions & Deletion

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of any account-related metadata, contact us through vastx.app or revoke app access directly in your Google Account security settings.
